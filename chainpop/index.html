ChainPop

A strategic chain-reaction puzzle where every tap counts. Lower the numbers, trigger precisely timed reactions, and clear the board before your moves—or the clock—run out.

Discover the game

Small taps. Huge reactions.

ChainPop starts simple, then steadily expands into larger boards and tougher decisions. Read the numbers, choose the right target, and turn one smart move into a satisfying cascade.

Think before you pop

Each board is a compact strategy challenge. Spend your limited moves carefully and make every reaction work for you.

Master special balls

Bombs, electric balls, rainbow balls, and durable blockers reshape the board and open new ways to win.

Rise through 1,000 levels

Boards grow gradually from approachable grids into intense challenges, with timed levels and fresh layouts along the way.

Build your best run

Earn stars and PopCoins, stock useful power-ups, customize your look, and compete for your highest level on Google Play Games.
