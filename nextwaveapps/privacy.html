This Privacy Policy explains how NextWave Apps (“NextWave Apps,” “we,” “us” or “our”) handles information when you use our mobile applications, games, websites and related services (collectively, the “Services”). Features differ by product, so some sections may not apply to every Service.

1. Information we handle

Information you provide

  • Messages and diagnostic details you send to support, such as the product name, device model and issue description.
  • Profile information you choose to provide through an account or platform service.
  • Choices, settings and content you submit through a Service where that feature is available.

Information generated through use

  • Gameplay and app progress, scores, achievements, virtual currency balances, settings and entitlement status.
  • Basic technical and diagnostic information such as device type, operating-system version, app version, crash details, performance events and approximate timestamps.
  • Advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interaction events when an ad-supported Service uses advertising.
  • Purchase status and product identifiers. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Local information

Some progress and settings may remain only on your device. Removing the app, clearing its storage or changing devices can remove local information unless the applicable Service offers cloud synchronization.

2. How we use information

We use information as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide, operate, maintain and improve the Services;
  • save progress, verify entitlements, process virtual items and enable requested features;
  • operate achievements, leaderboards, authentication and optional connected features;
  • show, measure and manage advertising where applicable;
  • detect crashes, abuse, fraud, cheating and security threats;
  • respond to support, privacy and legal requests; and
  • comply with law and enforce our agreements.

Depending on your location, our legal bases may include performance of a contract, consent, compliance with legal obligations and our legitimate interests in operating secure and reliable Services.

3. Service providers and third parties

Our Services may rely on third-party platforms that process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Depending on the product and features you use, these may include:

  • Google Play and Google Play Games Services for distribution, authentication, achievements, saved data or leaderboards;
  • Google AdMob for advertising and related measurement;
  • RevenueCat and app-store billing providers for purchase and subscription entitlement management;
  • hosting, analytics, crash-reporting and infrastructure providers needed to operate and protect the Services.

We do not sell personal information for money. Certain advertising or analytics activities may be treated as “sharing,” “targeted advertising” or a similar term under some laws. Available controls are described below.

4. Advertising

Some Services display banner, interstitial or rewarded advertisements. Advertising partners may process device identifiers, general device information, IP-derived approximate location, ad delivery events and interactions to provide, limit, secure and measure ads. Where required, we request consent or provide privacy choices. Device-level advertising controls may also be available through Android, Google settings or your regional consent interface.

Rewarded advertisements grant the stated in-app benefit only after the advertising provider confirms completion. Availability is not guaranteed and may depend on network access, eligibility and inventory.

5. Purchases and virtual items

Payments are processed by the applicable app store, not directly by NextWave Apps. We may receive product identifiers, transaction references, purchase state and entitlement information needed to deliver or restore an eligible purchase. Consumable virtual items generally cannot be restored after use unless required by law or expressly stated in the product.

6. Play Games, authentication and leaderboards

If you choose or permit Google Play Games sign-in, we may access the limited platform information required for the enabled feature, such as a player identifier, display name, profile image, scores, achievements and authentication status. We use it to identify your game profile, submit eligible scores and display platform features. Gameplay should remain available without connected services where the product supports offline play, though online leaderboards and similar features require connectivity.

Google API information is used only to provide or improve the user-facing feature for which access was granted, in accordance with applicable Google API Services policies. We do not use Google account information for unrelated advertising.

7. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including providing the Service, maintaining records, resolving disputes, preventing abuse and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type, feature and provider. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection, and to withdraw consent. You may also:

  • change app permissions through device settings;
  • manage Google Play Games and Google account connections through Google settings;
  • use available advertising privacy or device identifier controls;
  • decline optional sign-in features; and
  • request deletion of information controlled by us by emailing the address below.

To protect users, we may need to verify a request. We cannot delete information controlled solely by another provider; requests concerning that provider should be directed to it. Deleting cloud information does not necessarily remove local app data, which can usually be cleared through device settings.

9. Children

Our general-audience Services are not directed to children below the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information should contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. International processing

We and our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Those countries may have different data-protection rules. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as our Services, providers or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Material changes may also be communicated in the relevant Service or store listing where appropriate.
