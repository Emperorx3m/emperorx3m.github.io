The assignment

Every room is a decision.

Control the Warden, survive coordinated attackers and climb through an escalating tower. Floor 20 is the first complete milestone in the current test build—not the final ambition. Positioning matters: enemies pursue, turn, fire and close distance while you decide when to move, strike and commit.

01 / CONTROL

Fight your way

Use direct movement or the on-screen control. Switch camera styles when the room calls for a different read of the fight.

02 / PRESSURE

No harmless targets

Hostiles track the Warden, attack at range or punish contact. Crowds become a tactical problem, not scenery.

03 / PROGRESSION

Build between runs

Earn in-game credits, unlock equipment and improve the Warden. Progress and preferences are saved locally.

04 / VARIETY

Changing floors

New formations and hazards force adaptation as the tower escalates. Major encounters wait at key milestones.

05 / ATMOSPHERE

Serious downtime

A restrained military-industrial presentation with readable combat, short sessions and no cartoon overload.

06 / OWN THE RUN

Offline-first

The current test build requires no player account. Play state, loadout and settings remain on your device.
