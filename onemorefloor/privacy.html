Privacy Policy

Current-build statement: One More Floor is offline-first and does not require a NextWave player account. The game uses Google AdMob for advertising and consent management and RevenueCat with Google Play Billing to offer and restore a Remove Ads purchase.

1. Scope and who we are

This policy applies to the One More Floor mobile game, its official informational website and support communications relating to the game (the “Service”). The publisher is NextWave Apps (“NextWave,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). The Android application identifier is com.nextwaveapps.onemorefloor.

A store, operating-system provider, hosting company, email provider or linked third party may process information under its own policy. This notice describes NextWave’s practices for the current version.

3. Information the Service handles

3.1 Local game data

The game saves information needed to continue your experience: progress, in-game credits, owned and selected equipment, upgrades, performance statistics, and preferences such as sound, music, haptics, visual feedback, controls and camera view. It is associated with the app installation, not a NextWave player account.

Local data ordinarily remains on your device. Your operating system, backup service, transfer utility or device administrator may copy or restore it according to settings and policies controlled by you or that provider.

3.2 Advertising and consent data

Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, device and app information, diagnostics, consent selections, ad impressions, interactions and fraud-prevention signals. Available ad personalization depends on your region, consent choices, device settings and Google’s policies. Optional rewarded ads grant game credits only after the advertising service confirms completion.

3.3 Purchase and entitlement data

Google Play processes checkout and payment. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts, product and entitlement status, an app-user identifier generated for the installation, store information and related diagnostics so the game can verify or restore Remove Ads. NextWave does not receive your complete payment-card number.

3.4 Support messages

If you email us, we receive the address used, your message and anything you voluntarily attach. Send only what is necessary. Never send a password, full payment-card details, private keys, government identification or unrelated sensitive information.

3.5 Website delivery

These pages do not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. The host, network or browser may process ordinary technical records—such as IP address, request time, browser information and requested page—to deliver and protect the site.

3.6 Data not requested for gameplay

The game does not ask NextWave for your name, postal address, phone number, precise GPS location, contacts, media, camera input or microphone input. It does not send gameplay progress to a NextWave gameplay server.

4. How information is used

Local data is used to restore progress, apply settings, calculate unlocks and operate gameplay. Advertising data is used to request, deliver, measure and secure ads, respect consent choices and award optional rewarded-ad credits. Purchase data is used to complete, verify and restore Remove Ads. Support correspondence is used to answer you, investigate faults, protect the Service and comply with law.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may rely on consent, performance of a purchase contract, compliance with law, or legitimate interests such as operating, securing and supporting the Service. We do not sell support messages or use them to build advertising profiles.

5. When information may be shared

Local gameplay progress is not sent to a NextWave gameplay server. Information may be processed or disclosed as reasonably necessary:

  • by Google AdMob and its advertising partners to provide, measure and secure ads and manage consent;
  • by RevenueCat and Google Play to offer, verify, manage and restore purchases;
  • by providers supporting email, hosting, security or technical support;
  • to obey law or a valid governmental request, protect rights or Service integrity, or complete a business transfer with required safeguards; or
  • at your direction or with your consent.

These providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies and may process information in countries where they or their processors operate.

6. Device access and platform features

The game uses device storage for local saves, audio output for sounds and music, and vibration or haptic feedback when enabled. These can be adjusted in the game. Android may show standard technical permissions needed to install, update or run framework components; a platform permission does not itself mean the game actively collects personal information through it.

The current gameplay does not require camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access. If a later feature needs additional access, we will request permission where required and update disclosures before or when it becomes available.

7. Retention, reset and deletion

Local game data

Local data remains until overwritten, cleared through Android’s app-storage controls, removed by uninstalling, or removed by the device or operating system. A platform backup may remain depending on your settings. Clearing data can permanently erase progress, credits, settings, unlocks and statistics.

Support correspondence

We retain messages only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the matter, keep necessary records, prevent abuse or comply with law. Longer retention may apply to an active dispute, security issue or legal obligation. You may request deletion, subject to information we must retain or may lawfully preserve.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No device, email, transmission or storage method is completely secure. Protect your device, install legitimate updates and obtain the app only from trusted sources. Local saves are for continuity, not confidential storage.

9. Children’s privacy

One More Floor is a general-audience action game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information through the game from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent information to us should contact us for review and, where appropriate, deletion.

Age thresholds vary. A parent or guardian should supervise a minor’s use where required by law, platform rules or household policy.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You control local gameplay information through Android’s storage, backup and uninstall settings. You may manage advertising consent through any privacy message presented by Google and relevant device settings. Purchasing Remove Ads suppresses banner and timed interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available by choice. The app provides a Restore Purchase control.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain portability, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Requests concerning data controlled by Google, RevenueCat or Google Play may need to be directed to that provider. Rights may be limited by law and may require reasonable verification.

11. International use

You may use the Service from countries with different laws. Support email and web-hosting information may be processed where the relevant providers operate. Where required, lawful safeguards apply. Current local gameplay data is not transferred to a NextWave gameplay server.

12. Policy changes and future features

We may update this policy as the game or law changes. We will revise the date and provide additional notice where required. Material new collection—such as accounts, cloud leaderboards, analytics, advertising or real-money purchases—will be disclosed in an updated policy and, where required, in the app or store before or when it begins.
